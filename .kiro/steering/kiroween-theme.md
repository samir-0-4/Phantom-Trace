# Steering: Kiroween 2026 Theme (Frankenstein / Frankenstein's Monster Motif)

## Theme Vision
PhantomTrace participates in the **Kiroween 2026 Hackathon** under the **Frankenstein** category. The theme embodies the concept of a digital threat investigation platform "stitched together from disparate fragments":
- Multi-vector phishing artifacts (SMS, emails, PDF attachments, screenshots, voice transcripts) stitched into a unified forensic dossier.
- Six specialized AI forensic agents cooperating like distinct organs of a reasoning organism.
- Cross-case campaign reconstruction connecting seemingly disconnected "haunted" infrastructure.

## UI Design & Aesthetic Guidelines

### 1. Spooky Dark Aesthetic
- **Base Background:** Deep eerie obsidian (`#090d16` / `#0b0f19`).
- **Accent Glows:** Ghostly emerald/cyan spectral glow (`#10b981` / `#06b6d4`) and sinister crimson (`#f43f5e`) for critical threats.
- **Atmospheric Effects:** Subtle spectral fog and radial gradient glow on graph and header surfaces.

### 2. "Stitched from Many Parts" Motif
- **Stitched Borders:** Tasteful dashed/stitched border accents (`border-dashed border-emerald-500/30` or custom stitched borders) representing disparate forensic evidence and multi-agent synthesis.
- **Phantom Logo Mark:** Ghost/phantom icon paired with forensic crosshairs.

### 3. Phantom Microcopy
- Campaign matches describe "Haunted infrastructure detected across dossiers".
- Multi-agent orchestration describes "6 specialized analytical spirits assembled".
- Threat alerts indicate "Spectral threat presence identified".

### 4. Accessibility & Safety Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)
- **Contrast Standards:** All text elements must strictly adhere to WCAG AA contrast ratios ($\ge 4.5:1$ for normal text, $\ge 3:1$ for large text).
- **Reduced Motion:** All glow animations, pulsing edges, and atmospheric fog transitions must respect `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
- **Functionality First:** The spooky styling is a decorative, atmospheric layer. It must never compromise epistemic badge clarity (`VERIFIED`, `AI_INFERENCE`, `UNKNOWN`), graph readability, or report printing.
